# Exclusions And Future Scope

Not part of the focused T00 implementation:

- general GraphRAG benchmarking
- Microsoft GraphRAG, LightRAG, or HippoRAG integration
- semantic KG extraction, communities, PageRank, or graph database selection
- trained chunker routing or query-time rank-fusion benchmarking
- model training to generate provenance addresses
- LoRA or other model training
- a generalized Knowledge Unit ontology
- broad every-format implementation
- live internet capability discovery in ordinary CI

The schema and extension points must stay open for these later phases.
